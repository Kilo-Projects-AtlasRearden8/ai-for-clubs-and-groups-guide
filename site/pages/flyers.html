Designing Event Flyers with AI

Step-by-step beginner guide

Write event flyer copy using free AI. Print the planner at the end and fill it in by hand if you like.

You will need: a free AI chat tool, about 10–15 minutes, and fake example details (never real member names — see privacy rules).

Step 1: Decide the event

Write down on paper:

  • Club name
  • Event name
  • Date and time
  • Location
  • Cost (free, $5, etc.)
  • Who is welcome (everyone, members only, families, etc.)
  • One thing people should bring (optional)
  • How to RSVP or contact (club role or public email, not a personal phone)

Step 2: Choose the flyer's feeling

Decide the tone:

  • Friendly and welcoming
  • Exciting and energetic
  • Calm and informational

Tell the AI the tone in your prompt.

Step 3: Copy the example prompt

Open your free AI chat and paste this prompt. Replace the bracketed parts with your own non-private details.

You are a helpful assistant writing short event flyer copy for a community club. Write a flyer with these details: - Club name: [Example Garden Club] - Event: [Spring Plant Swap] - Date: [Saturday, April 18, 2026] - Time: [10:00 AM to 1:00 PM] - Location: [Community center, room B] - Cost: [Free] - Who is welcome: [Everyone, all ages] - Bring: [A plant or seed to swap, if you have one] - RSVP: [Email the club chair at gardenclub@example.org] Format: 1. A catchy headline (one line) 2. A friendly 2–3 sentence blurb 3. Bullet list: when, where, cost, who is welcome, what to bring 4. One RSVP line Tone: warm and welcoming, plain English, never condescending. Do not use real member names or phone numbers.

Step 4: Read and fix the draft

Check:

  • Are the date, time, and location correct?
  • Is the cost right?
  • Is the RSVP info a public club contact, not someone's personal cell?
  • Did the AI invent a door prize or speaker? Remove anything untrue.

Edit the draft yourself. You are the editor.

Step 5: Turn the copy into a flyer

Paste the final text into any tool you already use:

  • Word, Google Docs, or a free online flyer maker
  • Print and post on community bulletin boards
  • Share as an image or PDF in your club's existing group chat

Do not paste your member list into any design tool to "personalize" flyers.
