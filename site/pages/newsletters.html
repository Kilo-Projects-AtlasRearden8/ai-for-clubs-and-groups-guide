Writing Club Newsletters with AI

Step-by-step beginner guide

Draft club newsletters using free AI. Print the planner at the end and fill it in by hand if you like.

You will need: a free AI chat tool, about 15–20 minutes, and fake example details (never real member names — see privacy rules).

Step 1: Choose what the newsletter is about

Decide:

  • Club name
  • Issue date (for example, "April 2026")
  • Main news (one or two items)
  • Upcoming events
  • Who to contact (use a club role, not a personal name: "club chair" or "membership coordinator")

Step 2: Pick your sections

Newsletters work well with short sections:

  1. Header — club name, issue date
  2. Welcome note from the chair
  3. What happened last month
  4. Coming up soon
  5. Member spotlight (optional, keep it vague: "a member shares their garden")
  6. How to join or renew
  7. Contact line

Step 3: Copy the example prompt

Open your free AI chat and paste this prompt. Replace the bracketed parts with your own non-private details.

You are a helpful assistant writing short, friendly newsletters for a community club. Write a newsletter with these details: - Club name: [Example Garden Club] - Issue: [April 2026] - Main news: [Spring plant sale on April 18 raised $420 for the community garden] - Upcoming events: [Monthly meetup, first Saturday of every month, 10 AM, community center] - Contact: [club chair, gardenclub@example.org] Sections: 1. Welcome note from the chair — 2 short paragraphs 2. What happened last month — 3 short items 3. Coming up soon — 3 short items 4. How to join — 2 short sentences 5. Contact — 1 line Use plain English. Keep each section short. Friendly tone, never condescending. Do not include real member names or contact details.

Never use real member names, emails, or phone numbers. Refer to roles instead ("the treasurer," "a longtime member").

Step 4: Read and fix the draft

Check:

  • Are dates and times correct?
  • Is the club name spelled right?
  • Is the tone warm and plain?
  • Did the AI invent members or events? Remove anything made up.

Edit the draft yourself. You are the editor.

Step 5: Save and share

Save as a document, print it, or paste into your club's existing email or newsletter tool. Do not paste your member mailing list into AI to "personalize" each copy.
