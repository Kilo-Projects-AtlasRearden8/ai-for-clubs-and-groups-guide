Printable Planners

Plan away from the screen

Print these pages (or copy them into a notebook) to plan your club's AI work away from the screen. Nothing you write here ever goes into an AI tool.

Agenda planner

Use before you ask AI to draft a meeting agenda.

============================================================ CLUB MEETING AGENDA PLANNER ============================================================ CLUB: _______________________________ DATE: _______________ TIME: __________ LOCATION: ___________________________ CHAIR: _____________________________ GOAL OF MEETING: ____________________ _____________________________________ AGENDA ITEMS (in order, with times): 1. ________________________________ ____ min 2. ________________________________ ____ min 3. ________________________________ ____ min 4. ________________________________ ____ min 5. ________________________________ ____ min NOTES: ______________________________ _____________________________________

Newsletter planner

Use before you ask AI to draft a newsletter.

============================================================ CLUB NEWSLETTER PLANNER ============================================================ CLUB: _______________________________ ISSUE DATE: ________________________ MAIN NEWS (1–2 items): 1. ________________________________ 2. ________________________________ WHAT HAPPENED LAST MONTH: 1. ________________________________ 2. ________________________________ COMING UP SOON: 1. ________________________________ 2. ________________________________ HOW TO JOIN / RENEW: _______________ _____________________________________ CONTACT ROLE (not a personal name): _______________ EMAIL / PHONE: __________________________

Event flyer planner

Use before you ask AI to draft flyer copy.

============================================================ EVENT FLYER PLANNER ============================================================ CLUB: _______________________________ EVENT: _____________________________ DATE: _______________ TIME: __________ LOCATION: ___________________________ COST: _______________ WHO IS WELCOME: ____________________ BRING: _____________________________ RSVP CONTACT (role or public email): ____________________ TONE: [ ] Friendly [ ] Exciting [ ] Calm HEADLINE: ___________________________ _____________________________________ BLURB (2–3 sentences): _____________________________________ _____________________________________

Roster privacy sheet

Use on paper at sign-up events. Enter details yourself into a private spreadsheet later. Never photograph this sheet and send it to an AI tool.

============================================================ NEW MEMBER SIGN-UP (PAPER ONLY) ============================================================ CLUB: _______________________________ DATE: ______________________________ Name: ______________________________ Email: _____________________________ Phone: _____________________________ Dues paid: [ ] Yes [ ] No Keep this sheet in a locked drawer or private digital folder. Never send it to an AI tool.

Printing tips

  • Print in black and white; these pages are designed to be ink-friendly.
  • Fill in with pen or pencil, then follow the matching manual.
  • Keep completed planners in a folder only club officers can access.
