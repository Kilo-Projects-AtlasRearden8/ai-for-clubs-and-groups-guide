How to use each manual

  1. Read the "You will need" box — usually just a free AI chat tool.
  2. Work through the numbered steps in order.
  3. Copy the example prompt, replace the fake details with your own (non-private) details, and paste it into the AI chat.
  4. Read the AI's draft carefully. AI makes mistakes. You are the editor.
  5. Print or save the final version. Use the planner sheet if you want to work on paper first.

Golden rule: Never paste real member data — names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, or dues — into an AI tool. Use made-up examples instead. See Privacy rules.
