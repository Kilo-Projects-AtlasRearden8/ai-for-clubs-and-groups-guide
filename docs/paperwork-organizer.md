# Paperwork Organizers for Clubs

A beginner guide to keeping club paperwork organized using AI and simple tools.
Printable planners included.

## What club paperwork includes

- Membership applications
- Meeting minutes
- Event flyers and sign-up sheets
- Financial records
- Club policies and bylaws

## A simple filing system

Use folders — either paper or digital — organized by category:

```
Club Records/
├── Membership/
│   ├── blank-application-template.pdf
│   └── member-list (keep offline, private)
├── Meetings/
│   ├── agenda-template.md
│   └── minutes-2026-03.md
├── Events/
│   ├── flyer-template.md
│   └── sign-up-sheet-template.pdf
└── Finances/
    ├── dues-tracker-template.csv
    └── budget-2026.md
```

## How AI helps

- Draft templates for forms and sign-up sheets
- Explain how to organize files
- Suggest naming conventions (e.g., `minutes-YYYY-MM-DD.md`)
- Write cover letters or policy drafts

## Privacy reminder

Keep real membership lists and financial records offline or in a password-protected
folder. Never paste them into an AI tool.
