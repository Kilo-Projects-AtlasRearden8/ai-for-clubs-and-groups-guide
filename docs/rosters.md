# Tidying Member Rosters with AI (Privacy-Safe)

A beginner guide to cleaning up and organizing club rosters without putting
member data into AI. Read docs/privacy.md first.

## The golden rule

**Never paste a real member roster into an AI tool.** No names, emails, phone
numbers, or addresses. AI chats can store what you type, so a roster pasted
into a chat can expose your members' private information.

## What you can do safely

You can use AI to:

- Explain spreadsheet or on-paper steps ("How do I sort a list in Google
  Sheets?")
- Write instructions for removing duplicates by hand
- Suggest a folder or file-naming system for your club records
- Draft a welcome email template (fill in real details yourself, outside AI)

You must not:

- Upload a roster file to an AI site
- Paste member names or contact info into an AI chat
- Ask AI to "clean up" or "format" a real list

## Safe way to tidy a roster

### Step 1: Work on paper or in a local spreadsheet

Open a spreadsheet on your own computer (or use paper columns):

| First name | Last name | Email | Phone | Joined | Dues paid |
|------------|-----------|-------|-------|--------|-----------|

Do not upload this file anywhere.

### Step 2: Ask AI for instructions only

Paste this kind of prompt into a free AI chat:

```
I keep a club roster in a spreadsheet with columns for name, email, phone,
date joined, and dues paid. Explain in plain English, step by step, how to:
1. Sort by last name
2. Find duplicate email addresses
3. Highlight members whose dues are unpaid
Do not ask me to paste member data. Use fake examples only.
```

AI gives you steps. You do the work in your own spreadsheet.

### Step 3: Keep a backup

Before making changes, save a copy of the roster file with today's date in the
name (for example, `roster-2026-03-14.csv`). Keep backups in a folder only
club officers can access.

### Step 4: Share safely

When you need to send the roster, use your club's existing secure channel.
Do not email it to AI tools or post it in public chats.

## Printable roster planner

Use this on paper to collect info at sign-up, then enter it yourself into a
spreadsheet offline:

```
CLUB: _______________________________
DATE COLLECTED: ____________________

NEW MEMBER SIGN-UP SHEET
Name: ______________________________
Email: _____________________________
Phone: _____________________________
Dues paid: [ ] Yes [ ] No
How they heard about us: _______________

Keep this sheet in a locked drawer or a private digital folder.
Never photograph it and send the photo to an AI tool.
```
