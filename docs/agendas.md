# Writing Meeting Agendas with AI

A step-by-step beginner guide to drafting club meeting agendas using free AI.
Print the planner at the end and fill it in by hand if you like.

## You will need

- A free AI chat tool (see docs/free-models.md)
- About 10 minutes
- Fake example details (never real member names — see docs/privacy.md)

## Steps

### Step 1: Decide what the meeting is about

Before you open the AI, write down on paper:

- Club name
- Meeting date and time
- Where it meets (room, Zoom link, etc.)
- Who runs the meeting (chair or facilitator)
- Main goal of the meeting (one sentence)

### Step 2: List the agenda items

Jot down what needs to be discussed, in order:

1. Welcome and introductions
2. Approve last meeting's notes
3. Officer or committee reports
4. Old business (unfinished items)
5. New business
6. Upcoming events
7. Next meeting date
8. Adjournment

Give each item a rough time (for example, "Welcome — 5 minutes").

### Step 3: Copy the example prompt

Open your free AI chat and paste this prompt. Replace the bracketed parts with
your own non-private details.

```
You are a helpful assistant writing meeting agendas for a community club.

Write a meeting agenda with these details:
- Club name: [Example Garden Club]
- Meeting date: [Saturday, March 14, 2026]
- Meeting time: [10:00 AM]
- Location: [Community center, room B]
- Chair: [facilitator]
- Goal: [plan the spring plant sale]

Agenda items with times:
1. Welcome and introductions — 5 minutes
2. Approve last meeting's notes — 5 minutes
3. Treasurer report — 10 minutes
4. Spring plant sale planning — 20 minutes
5. Upcoming events — 10 minutes
6. Next meeting date — 5 minutes

Use plain English. Keep it friendly and brief. Include a header with the club
name, date, time, and location.
```

Never use real member names or contact info. If a report mentions a person,
write "Treasurer report" instead of "Maria's report."

### Step 4: Read and fix the draft

AI drafts are starting points, not finished documents. Check:

- Are the times right?
- Is the club name spelled correctly?
- Is the tone friendly, not stiff?
- Did the AI add anything you did not ask for?

Edit the draft yourself. You are the editor; AI is only the first draft.

### Step 5: Save and share

Save the final agenda as a document or print it. Send it to members before the
meeting by the channel your club already uses (email, group chat, printed
handout). Do not paste the member list into AI to "format" it.
