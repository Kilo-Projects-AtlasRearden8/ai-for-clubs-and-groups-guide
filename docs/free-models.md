# Free AI Models You Can Use Today

You do not need to pay for AI to run a club. This page lists no-cost models
and ways to reach them, plus how to pick the right one for each task.

## What "free model" means here

A free model is an AI you can chat with at no charge. Some have daily limits
(after so many messages you wait until tomorrow). That is fine for club work.

## Where to find free models

| Option | What it is | Good for |
|--------|------------|----------|
| Web chat (no account) | Some AI sites let you chat without signing up | Quick drafts, one-off tasks |
| Free-tier chat apps | Sign up with an email, get a daily message limit | Newsletters, agendas, flyers |
| Local models | Run an AI on your own computer, no internet needed | Privacy-sensitive drafts, rosters |
| Mobile apps | Free AI apps for phone or tablet | Drafting on the go |

Examples change often, so search for "free AI chat" and pick a well-known
service. Look for ones that say "free" or "no credit card required."

## How to pick a model for each club task

| Task | Suggested model type | Why |
|------|---------------------|-----|
| Meeting agenda | Any free text chat | Simple structure, quick draft |
| Newsletter | Free chat with longer replies | More room for sections and tone |
| Roster cleanup | Local model or privacy-safe chat | Member info stays on your device |
| Event flyer | Any free text chat | Short, punchy copy works well |

## Tips for better results

- Be specific: "Write a 200-word friendly newsletter for a gardening club"
  works better than "Write a newsletter."
- Say who is reading: beginners, seniors, teens, families.
- Ask for a draft, not a final: "Draft an agenda, then I will edit it."
- If the first result is off, ask again: "Make it shorter," "Make it more
  formal," or "Add a welcome section."

## Stay local-first when you can

Running a model on your own computer means nothing leaves your machine. Good
for rosters and anything with member names. See docs/rosters.md for safe ways
to handle lists.
