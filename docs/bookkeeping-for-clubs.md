# Bookkeeping for Clubs with AI

A beginner guide to managing club finances and dues with AI assistance.
Education only — not a substitute for a professional accountant.

## What club bookkeeping involves

- Tracking dues payments
- Recording expenses (supplies, venue, events)
- Keeping a simple budget
- Preparing reports for members

## What AI can help with

- Explaining bookkeeping concepts in plain English
- Suggesting spreadsheet layouts
- Drafting financial reports
- Categorizing expenses

## What AI should NOT do

- Handle real money or bank access
- File taxes or legal documents
- Give legal or accounting advice
- Store sensitive financial data

## Step-by-step: set up a simple dues tracker

### Step 1: Create a spreadsheet

Make columns like:

| Member | Dues Amount | Date Paid | Payment Method | Notes |

### Step 2: Ask AI for a report template

Paste into a free AI chat:

```
I run a small community club. Create a simple monthly financial report
template in plain English. Include sections for: income (dues), expenses
(supplies, venue), balance, and notes. Keep it beginner-friendly.
```

### Step 3: Fill it in yourself

Enter your own numbers into the spreadsheet. Do not upload the file to an AI
tool if it contains real member names or payment details.

## Privacy reminder

Never paste real member names, payment amounts, or bank details into an AI
tool. Use fake examples like "Member A paid $10 on March 1."
