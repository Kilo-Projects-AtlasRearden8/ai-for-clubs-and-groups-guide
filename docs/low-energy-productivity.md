# Low-Energy Productivity for Club Organizers

A beginner guide to managing club tasks when you have limited energy.
ADHD-friendly and written for anyone who finds traditional productivity advice
too demanding.

## What "low-energy" means

Some days you have a lot of energy. Other days you do not. This guide is for
the low-energy days — small steps that keep your club moving without burning
you out.

## The tiny-steps method

Break every club task into steps that take 5 minutes or less:

| Big task | 5-minute step |
|----------|--------------|
| Plan annual calendar | List 3 upcoming events |
| Write newsletter | Draft one paragraph |
| Update roster | Add one new member |
| Design flyer | Write the headline only |

## How AI helps on low-energy days

- **Too tired to write?** Ask AI to draft it, then just edit.
- **Can't start?** Ask AI for the first three steps.
- **Overwhelmed by email?** Ask AI to suggest a short reply.
- **Forgetting what to do next?** Ask AI to make a simple checklist.

## Example: low-energy newsletter workflow

1. Open AI chat. Ask: "Draft a 3-sentence club newsletter welcome." (2 minutes)
2. Copy the draft into your email tool. (1 minute)
3. Add date and event details. (2 minutes)
4. Send. (1 minute)

Total: 6 minutes. You are done.

## Printable low-energy planner

```
CLUB: _______________________________

TODAY I CAN DO (pick 1–3):
[ ] One AI draft (agenda, newsletter, or flyer)
[ ] One email reply
[ ] One roster update
[ ] Five minutes of filing

ENERGY LEVEL: [ ] Low  [ ] Medium  [ ] High

IF ENERGY IS LOW:
- Do one 5-minute task only.
- Stop when the timer rings.
- Rest is productive too.

```
