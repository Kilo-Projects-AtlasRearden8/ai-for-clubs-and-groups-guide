# Privacy Rules for Clubs Using AI

**Read this first. It is the most important page in this guide.**

## The golden rule

**Never paste real member data into an AI tool.**

That means no:

- Member names
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Home addresses
- Dues or payment info
- Attendance or health details

## Why

When you type into most AI chats, that text can be stored, reviewed, or used to
train future models. A member list pasted into a chat becomes data the AI
company might keep. Protect your members' privacy by keeping their details out
of AI tools entirely.

## What to do instead

Use made-up examples in every prompt.

| Instead of this | Try this |
|-----------------|----------|
| "Email Maria Chen at maria.chen@email.com about dues" | "Email a member about dues, keep it friendly" |
| "Remind the 12 members on my list about Saturday" | "Remind a dozen members about Saturday's event" |
| "Write a flyer for the Main Street book club" | "Write a flyer for a neighborhood book club" |

If you need to clean up a real roster, do it on paper or in a spreadsheet on
your own computer. Do not upload the file to an AI site. Local models (run on
your own machine) are safer, but when in doubt, keep member data away from AI
completely.

## If something goes wrong

If you accidentally paste real member data:

1. Delete the chat if the tool allows it.
2. Do not share that chat link with anyone.
3. Tell your club's organizer so they can decide whether members need to know.
