# AI Aids for Healthcare Workers (Education Only)

A beginner guide to using AI for healthcare-worker administrative and
educational tasks. **This is not medical advice.** Never use AI for patient
diagnosis, treatment decisions, or clinical documentation involving real
patient data.

## What this guide covers

- Shift scheduling and time-off requests
- Continuing education notes
- Study aids for certifications
- Staff meeting agendas
- Wellness and burnout resources

## What this guide does NOT cover

- Patient diagnosis or treatment
- Medical documentation with real patient names or data
- Clinical decision-making
- Prescribing or dosing information

## The golden rule for healthcare

**Never paste real patient data into an AI tool.** No names, dates of birth,
medical record numbers, diagnoses, or treatment details. Use made-up examples
only.

## Step-by-step: create a study aid

Say you are preparing for a certification exam.

### Step 1: Choose a topic

Pick one subject area, for example "vital signs review" or "medication
safety basics."

### Step 2: Ask AI for practice questions

Paste into a free AI chat:

```
Create 5 multiple-choice practice questions about vital signs for a nursing
student. Include answer key with brief explanations. Use plain English.
Do not include real patient cases or data.
```

### Step 3: Review and study

Read the questions and answers. AI can make mistakes — double-check against
your textbook or course materials.

## Step-by-step: draft a staff meeting agenda

### Step 1: List topics

- Schedule changes
- New protocols
- Staff wellness
- Upcoming training

### Step 2: Ask AI to format it

```
Write a 30-minute staff meeting agenda for a clinic team. Include: welcome,
schedule updates, new protocols, staff wellness check-in, and next steps.
Keep it professional but warm. Plain English.
```
